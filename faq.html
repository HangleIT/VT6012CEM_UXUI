Tickets

3 Qs
Q6

Can I buy tickets for different movies at the same time?

No. You may only buy tickets for one movie session at a time.

Q7

How many tickets can I purchase through online ticketing?

There is a maximum purchase limit of 8 tickets for each order.

Q12

Do I need to bring my own 3D glasses to watch 3D movies?

You may bring your own 3D glasses or buy them at the cinema box office.

Discounts

2 Qs
Q2

Does Newport Circuit offer child / senior / student discount?

Yes. Child, senior and student discount tickets are available for eligible audiences.

Q3

Can I use coupons or discounts through online ticketing?

Vouchers or discounts are not accepted through online ticketing. Please ask at the box office.

Payment

3 Qs
Q1

What payment methods do you accept online?

We currently accept Mastercard and Visa for online purchasing.

Q4

Do I need to pay any service charge for using online ticketing?

The service charge per ticket for online ticketing is HK$6 or more.

Q13

When is the closing time for online purchasing for each show?

Online purchasing usually closes 30 minutes before the show starts.

Collection

3 Qs
Q9

How do I collect my tickets?

Bring the same credit card used for the order and collect your tickets at the selected cinema.

Q10

If I have 2 or more transactions, do I need to pick up tickets separately?

No. You can collect all ordered tickets together at the same time.

Q11

Can I pick up the ordered tickets at any theatres of Newport Circuit?

No. Tickets should be collected at the theatre chosen during booking.

Refunds

3 Qs
Q5

When will my credit card be charged, and can I change or cancel the order?

Once the transaction is complete, your card will be charged immediately. Tickets are normally non-refundable and non-changeable.

Q8

What should I do if I purchased the wrong tickets after payment?

Tickets cannot be cancelled or changed after payment has been confirmed.

Q15

Can I refund the tickets when typhoon or rainstorm warning is issued?

Please contact the cinema box office with your ticket or credit card for assistance.
